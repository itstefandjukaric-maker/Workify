WORKIFY / HOW IT WORKS
A new job. A new place. A clearer way forward. With Workify, follow your next opportunity from the first search to your first day.

01 / CREATE YOUR PROFILE
Start by creating your free worker account. Enter your name and email, choose a password, then set your country and language. In your profile, add your trade, experience, and the languages you speak. Upload your CV and relevant documents. You choose what to share with each application.

02 / EXPLORE OPPORTUNITIES
Now explore jobs by trade and destination. Open a listing and look beyond the salary. Is the pay net or gross? How many hours will you work? Is accommodation included, and who pays the travel costs? Compare the details before deciding which opportunity is right for you.

03 / SEND YOUR APPLICATION
When you find a suitable role, select Apply. Choose the documents you want the company to see, and add a short message about your experience and availability. Review your details, then send your application. Your dashboard brings your applications and next steps together.

04 / MESSAGES & INTERVIEW
Use messages to ask questions and clarify the offer. When an interview is arranged, check the date, time, and time zone. Prepare a few questions, and be ready to explain your experience. Keep important details in your application conversation, so you can return to them later.

05 / REVIEW YOUR CONTRACT
Before accepting, read the contract carefully. Compare it with the original listing: salary, working hours, accommodation, and any deductions. Ask about anything that is unclear, and keep a signed copy. A clear agreement now helps you arrive knowing what to expect.

06 / DOCUMENTS & PERMITS
Follow progress in your application. Your agency adds updates and supporting documents for the visa or work permit process. These updates are supplied by the agency; they are not independent confirmation from government systems. Dates are estimates, so confirm the required approvals before booking your journey.

07 / TRAVEL & YOUR FIRST DAY
Before you travel, work through your checklist. Confirm your documents, accommodation, arrival contact, and first working day. Save copies of the important information. When you arrive, meet your team and follow the workplace safety instructions.

08 / WORK & YOUR FIRST PAY
Once you start, keep a record of your hours and check your first salary against your contract. Raise any concerns early. Workify is free for workers. If someone asks you to pay to get a job through Workify, report it.
