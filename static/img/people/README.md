# Workify people imagery — 22 October 2026

These three illustrative photographs were created with the built-in Imagegen tool for this visual refresh. They depict fictional people, not Workify customers, employees, verified employers or endorsements. They are decorative and have empty alternative text in the page markup.

- `home-workers.webp`: construction and maintenance professionals, used in the homepage's dedicated image column.
- `employer-team.webp`: an operations manager planning with an industrial team, used on the employer entry page.
- `agency-candidates.webp`: recruitment consultants meeting a candidate, used on the agency entry page.

Each image includes a 640px `-sm.webp` variant. The larger images retain the generated aspect ratio and are encoded as WebP at quality 83. Original generated PNG files are kept outside the website checkout. Exact generation prompts are retained in `prompts.json`.

The two smaller homepage photos (`photos/chef-sm.jpg` and `photos/furniture-sm.jpg`) are existing licensed stock assets documented in `../photos/README.md`.
