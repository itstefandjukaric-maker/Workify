About the job

Reinforcement work for residential and metro station projects in Riyadh. Work stops during the hottest midday hours in summer, as the law requires. The company provides camp accommodation, a monthly food allowance of SAR 300, transport, visa, Iqama, medical insurance and flights. Contracts are registered on Qiwa.

Requirements

  • 2 years as a steel fixer
  • Able to read bar bending schedules
  • Safety training (we repeat it on arrival)

About Riyadh

Riyadh is the capital of Saudi Arabia and a city of about 7 million people in the middle of the desert. Huge construction projects, factories and services need workers. Summers are dry and very hot (over 43 °C), winters cool. Alcohol is forbidden and religious rules are strict; Friday is the holy day.

  • Summer: around 43 °C during the day
  • Winter: around 20 °C during the day
