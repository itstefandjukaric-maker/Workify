  1. 1
    Step 1 of 8

    Make your free profile

    Write your trade, your experience and the languages you speak. Add your passport and CV once. You decide which company sees them.

  2. 2
    Step 2 of 8

    Find a real job

    Every job shows the pay, net or gross, the housing and what the employer pays for. Photos, a video and a map show the place.

  3. 3
    Step 3 of 8

    Apply in one tap

    Choose the documents you want to share. Only checked companies can publish jobs, and you never pay a fee.

  4. 4
    Step 4 of 8

    Chat and have the interview

    Write with the agency in your language. Pick an interview time; you see it on your own clock.

  5. 5
    Step 5 of 8

    Read the contract

    Compare the contract with the ad: pay, hours, housing. Workify can explain it in your language. Never sign what you do not understand.

  6. 6
    Step 6 of 8

    Visa and work permit

    The employer applies for your work permit, and the embassy gives the visa. Every step shows in your account.

  7. 7
    Step 7 of 8

    Travel

    Go through your checklist before you fly. Many employers pay the ticket; the job ad tells you.

  8. 8
    Step 8 of 8

    Start work and get paid

    Your first working day, then your first salary. Tell other workers your story, so they can trust what they read.
