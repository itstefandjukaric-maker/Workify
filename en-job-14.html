About the job

Production of equipment for the food industry: welding pipes and tanks made of stainless steel (TIG/141). Shift work Monday to Friday, overtime is paid. We help you find a flat near the company; rent is not included in the salary. We organise the visa process and the recognition of your qualification free of charge.

Requirements

  • At least 3 years of TIG welding experience
  • EN ISO 9606-1 certificate (or ready to take the test)
  • Reading technical drawings

About München

Munich is the capital of Bavaria and one of Germany's richest cities: car makers, engineering, food industry and hospitals. Rents are the highest in Germany, so most employers help with housing. Public transport is excellent. German is needed for most jobs; many employers pay for courses.
