Terms for companies

The rules every agency and employer accepts before publishing on Workify.

Draft text for review by a lawyer before launch. Not legally binding yet.

Version: 2026-10

Who these terms are for

These terms apply to recruitment agencies and employers (“companies”) that use Workify. The person who registers confirms that they may act for the company.

No fees for workers

Companies must not charge workers any fee or cost for recruitment – not for the job, the interview, the contract, the visa, the work permit, the ticket or the medical check – directly, through deductions from wages or through others. A breach closes the account at once.

Verification

Companies send their company registration and, for agencies, their recruitment licence. Workify checks them before the first job is published and may ask for them again at any time. Invalid documents close the account.

Honest job ads

Every ad shows the real pay and says whether it is net or gross, the housing and its cost, and what the employer pays for. Timelines are estimates and must be realistic. Photos must show the real workplace and housing, without business names, signs or addresses.

Workers' data

Companies use workers' data and documents only for the application they were shared for, keep them safe and delete them when they are no longer needed. Contacting workers outside the platform to avoid these terms is not allowed.

Prices

Job ads cost €29 per published job per month; the agency subscription costs €149 per month. Prices are without VAT. Invoices are sent by e-mail and are due within 14 days by bank transfer.

Placement fee and guarantee

When a worker hired through Workify signs the contract, the company owes one month's pay of that job (the lower end of the advertised pay), in the job's currency, minus any loyalty discount. If the worker leaves within 5 days of starting, 70 % of the fee is refunded; within 20 days, 30 %. Instead of a refund, the company can choose one replacement without a new fee within 30 days. The company reports the departure in the application.

Suspension

Workify may hide ads or suspend a company that breaks these terms, misleads workers or receives repeated justified reports. Fees already due stay due; fees for hires that do not happen are cancelled.

Contact

Questions about these terms or about invoices: support@example.com.
