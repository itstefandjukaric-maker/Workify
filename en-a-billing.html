Placement fee and guarantee

Your discount: 10 %

When a worker signs the contract, the fee is one month's pay of that job (the lower end of the advertised pay). If the worker leaves soon after starting, part of the fee comes back:

  1. Leaves within 5 days70 % back
  2. Leaves within 20 days30 % back
  3. Leaves after 30 daysthe fee is kept

Instead of money back, you can choose a replacement worker without a new fee if the worker leaves within 30 days.

Discount for returning companies: −10 % after 3 hires, −20 % after 10 hires.
