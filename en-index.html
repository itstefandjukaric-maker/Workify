Questions workers ask

Do I pay anything to find a job here?

No. Workify is free for workers, and no agency on Workify may charge you – not for the job, the interview, the contract or the visa process. If anyone asks you for money, report the job.

How do I know a job is real?

Every agency sends us its company registration and recruitment licence before it can publish. Every job shows the pay and the employer. Check the agency's licence in your own country too – our guide shows where.

Which countries can I work in?

Jobs are in the Gulf countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain), in Europe and in the Western Balkans. The destinations page shows where jobs are open now.

Who pays for the visa and the flight?

Each job lists what the employer pays for or provides: visa and work permit, flight, housing, food, medical check. If something is not clear, ask the agency in the chat before you accept an offer.

Who sees my passport and documents?

Only the agencies you apply to, and only the documents you choose for that application. When the application closes, they lose access.

Can I use Workify in my language?

Yes. Workify works in 13 languages, including Hindi, Nepali, Bangla, Russian, Uzbek, Tajik, Georgian, Arabic, Swahili and French. Change the language with the globe button at the top.

I am an employer. How do I start?

Register your company and upload your registration and recruitment licence. After we verify them, you can publish jobs and search worker profiles.

How does it work, step by step?

Make a free profile, apply to a job and talk with the agency in the chat. Then come the interview (often by video), the offer and the contract, the visa or work permit, the journey and your first day. Every step shows in your account. A short film shows it all: How Workify works.

There is no job for me yet. What can I do?

Join the waiting list for up to 5 countries. Companies hiring there can find your profile, and you get a message when a new job opens there. There is no queue number and no lottery: companies choose who they invite.

Is the pay on the ad net or gross?

Every ad says whether the pay is net (what you receive) or gross (before tax and social insurance). In the Gulf, pay is usually net. In Europe, ads often show gross pay together with the agency's estimate of the net pay.

How do I check my contract?

Compare the contract with the job ad: pay, hours, housing, contract length and who pays the visa and the ticket. The agency adds the contract to your application, and you can have it explained and read aloud in your language. Never sign what you do not understand.

Can I see the workplace, the housing and the city?

Yes. Many ads have photos of the workplace and the housing and a short video from the employer. Every job page shows the city on a map with its weather and the time difference to your country. The map view shows all jobs by city.
